## The feel
Confident, punchy, information-dense. Forest-green `#1f3d2e` cards with cream `#f3eedf` text and gold
`#d9b45a` accents, Instrument Serif italics, giant serif titles that sit BEHIND her head (speaker cut-out),
mock app UI (typing chat bars, phone tap-through with a cursor, Follow button, group chat), checklists that
build row by row, count-ups. Motion bounces (`back.out`), cards arrive on the spoken word.

## Sound
Sound effects only (her choice, 2026-10-20): booms on hero titles, whooshes on card entrances, pops on
rows/chips, clicks on cursor taps, typing under typed text, ticks on counters, a riser into the key number.
On long videos keep booms to section titles only.
